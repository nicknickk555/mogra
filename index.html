Influence of geometry & practical guidelines

Empty cone

Rp = 0 and J is maximal. Concentration falls sharply through air; pruning is early and hard.

early · sharp · intense branching

Half-filled cone

Rp, Ra and Rh balance. The gradient softens and s* stays near the interface.

balanced · optimal · readable

Three-quarter fill

Rp dominates and J decreases. Moisture remains high longer; pruning shifts outward.

delayed · soft · smooth transition

Practical guidelines

  • Aim for approximately 50% fill and let gravity settle the mix.
  • Use a friable, airy texture and avoid excess fines.
  • Maintain gentle crossflow and keep the aperture clear.
  • Look for soft branching around the cone midsection.

When to deviate

  • Dry ambient: increase fill toward 60%.
  • Humid ambient: reduce fill to 20–30%.
  • Sensitive species: prefer 40–60% fill.
  • Avoid 75%+ for standard use.

Quick field check

If the cone wall feels damp beyond mid-height, reduce fill next cycle.

White, bushy laterals inside the cone indicate the optimal band.
